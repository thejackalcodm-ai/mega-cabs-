Help & usage guide

A quick walkthrough of everything Mega City Cab can do.

1. Register as a customer

Go to Register and fill in your name, address, NIC, and a username/password. On success you'll be given a registration number (e.g. REG-A1B2C3D4) — keep it, you'll need it to make a booking.

2. Login

Once registered, use Login with the username and password you chose. Logged-in status is shown in the navigation bar; use Logout there to end your session.

3. Book a cab

Go to Book a cab and enter your registration number, passenger details, destination, and pick an available vehicle. Submitting creates a booking with a generated order number (e.g. ORD-9F3B2C1A) and reserves that vehicle so no one else can book it at the same time.

4. View a booking & calculate the bill

Find your booking under Bookings and open it to see its details. From there, choose Calculate bill, enter any discount, and a bill is generated with a 10% tax applied on top of the vehicle's daily rate. The bill page has a Print bill button for a print-friendly copy.

5. Manage vehicles & drivers

Staff can add and view the fleet under Vehicles and Drivers. A vehicle's status (Available / Booked) updates automatically as bookings are made.

Troubleshooting

Every form shows an inline error message if something's wrong (e.g. a duplicate NIC, an unavailable vehicle, or a booking that's already been billed) — correct the highlighted field and resubmit.
